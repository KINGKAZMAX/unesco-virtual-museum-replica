Regions

The design of the entrance hall of the museum represents the continents of the world — all connected in the protection of our common heritage. Journey through the regions and discover how trafficking affects every part of the globe.

A global loss

Every region, every era

The museum features cultural objects from all regions and of various types — from manuscripts to sculptures and paintings — and from different periods of history. The stolen objects represent a significant loss to the cultural heritage of the submitting States.

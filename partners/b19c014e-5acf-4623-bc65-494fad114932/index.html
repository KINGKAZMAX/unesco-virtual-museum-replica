Our partners

Partner With Us

Responding to global challenges such as the illicit trafficking of cultural property requires strong, coordinated and inclusive action.

UNESCO engages with a broad array of partners on programme implementation, resource mobilisation, knowledge exchange and shared learning, joint advocacy, facilitation and coordination — thus strengthening the professional community, fostering knowledge, creating innovation and responding to emerging cultural heritage preservation needs.

Whereas stolen cultural objects displayed in the UNESCO Virtual Museum can only be submitted by UNESCO Member States, we are open to multiple avenues of collaboration, including research, capacity building, outreach or editorial support.

To formalise a partnership with UNESCO, governments, multilateral organisations, technical partners, the private sector and foundations, the art market, museums, academia — including UNESCO Chairs and UNITWIN — the Associated Schools network, as well as non-governmental organisations can engage through various channels with us and propose projects. These include, for example, supporting temporary exhibitions, helping curate content or collect testimonies of communities, sharing good practices, co-developing educational materials or contributing resources and expertise. The UNESCO Museum, described as a "living museum", offers a unique platform for such engagement. It is designed to evolve through contributions from diverse stakeholders, serving as a hub for dialogue, knowledge exchange, and the development of new tools to combat cultural property trafficking.

Partners can also engage through co-financing, in-kind support or strategic alliances that amplify impact. Interested organisations should explore UNESCO's Partnerships portal to understand the criteria and processes available at UNESCO. By joining forces with UNESCO, partners not only contribute to safeguarding cultural heritage, but also become part of a global movement that defends the protection of our cultural heritage, fosters resilience and promotes peace across borders.

For queries regarding partnerships, please contact convention1970@unesco.org
